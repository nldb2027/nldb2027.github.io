Each accepted paper must be covered by at least one author registration at the *regular* rate, even if that author is a student, completed by 23 April 2027<span class="dagger" title="Provisional">†</span>.

## Visa letters {#visa}

If you need a visa to travel to Romania, we issue an invitation letter once your registration is complete and paid. Write to <span class="ph">email</span> with your full name as in your passport, nationality, passport number and the title of your paper, if any.

<!-- aside:start -->

Visa procedures can take several weeks: register early. [More on visas.](/venue/#practical)
