## Format {#format}

- Use Springer's [LNCS template, LaTeX or Word](https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors), without changing margins or font sizes.
- Submit a single <span class="sc">pdf</span>.
- Respect the page limit for your paper type, including references and appendices.

### Review {#review}

Each submission is reviewed by at least three members of the programme committee, under double-blind review. Decisions are sent on 26 March 2027.

## Policies {#policies}


Submissions must be original and must not be under review at, or accepted by, another venue with archival published proceedings. Authors should follow the LNCS format. Submissions that do not adhere to these requirements will be desk-rejected.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register at the regular rate by the author registration deadline and present the paper in person at the conference.

Springer's editorial policies apply: a large language model cannot be listed as an author, and any use of such tools beyond copy-editing should be documented in the paper.
