§ 2

Anywhere on Earth

AoE is the last time zone on the planet to finish a given day. A deadline of 23:59 AoE has passed only once that day is over everywhere: if it is still 19 February anywhere on Earth, you are on time.

In practice: the paper deadline of 19 February 2027, 23:59 AoE, is 11:59 on 20 February utc, and 13:59 on 20 February in Bucharest.

Submission systems slow down in the last hours before a deadline; plan to submit a day early.
