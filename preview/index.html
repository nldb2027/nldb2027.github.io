§ 3

Call for papers

NNLDB invites original work on natural language and information systems, from models of meaning to systems that answer questions over data. Accepted papers are published by Springer in Lecture Notes in Computer Science.

topics include, but are not limited to

  1. Question answering, dialogue and interactive systems
  2. Interactivity and natural-language interfaces
  3. Generative models and large language models
  4. Information retrieval and text mining
  5. Discourse and pragmatics, sentiment analysis, argument mining
  6. Interpretability and model analysis in NLP
  7. Explainable AI
  8. AI safety and ethics
  9. Multimodality
  10. Social media and web data
  11. Efficient and low-resource methods
  12. Big data and scalability
  13. NLP applications

Read the full call for papers

main track. Novel and significant research on theory, algorithms, applications, architectures and resources; surveys and discussion papers.

industry track. Industrial-strength NLP work, case studies, reports on barriers to adoption, experience reports.

length. full papers up to 15 pages, short papers up to 11 pages, demo papers up to 6 pages, in lncs format, references included.

§ 4

Bucharest

NLDB comes to Romania for the first time. Bucharest, the country’s capital and largest city, is well connected by air to the rest of Europe; the conference is hosted by university.
