Bucharest

/ˌbuːkəˈrɛst/ n. (Romanian București [bukuˈreʃtʲ])

The capital of Romania, on the Dâmbovița river; host city of NLDB 2027.

§ 2

Getting there

By air

Henri Coandă International Airport (otp), in Otopeni, is about 17 km north of the city centre.

  • Train. Trains run from the airport station to Bucharest North railway station (Gara de Nord) about every 40 minutes; the journey takes around 20 minutes.
  • Bus. Express buses link the airport with the city centre; check the current lines with stb, the city’s transport company.
  • Taxi. Order one at the official terminals in the arrivals hall, or use a ride-hailing app.

By rail

International and domestic trains arrive at Gara de Nord, on metro lines M1 and M4.

Timetables change; check before you travel.

§ 3

Getting around

The metro has five lines (M1–M5); buses, trams and trolleybuses cover the rest of the city. A contactless bank card works as a ticket on the metro and on surface transport. The historic centre, Calea Victoriei and the Athenaeum are close together and best seen on foot.
