§ 1

Format

  • Use Springer’s lncs template, LaTeX or Word, without changing margins or font sizes.
  • Submit a single pdf.
  • Respect the page limit for your paper type; references and appendices count towards it.
TypeMaximum length
Full paper15 pages
Short paper11 pages
Demo paper6 pages

Papers that do not follow the format or exceed the limit may be rejected without review.

Springer lncs guidelines and templates

Limits as at NLDB 2025; to be confirmed.

§ 2

How to submit

Submissions are made through submission system; the link will appear here when submissions open. Choose the main or the industry track when you submit.

Questions about submission: email.

§ 3

Review

Each submission is reviewed by at least three members of the programme committee, under single- or double-blind review. Decisions are sent on 26 March 2027†.

§ 4

Policies

Originality

Submissions must be original and must not be under review at, or accepted by, another venue with published proceedings.

Presentation

At least one author of each accepted paper must register at the regular rate by the author registration deadline and present the paper in person at the conference.

Generative AI

Springer’s editorial policies apply: a large language model cannot be listed as an author, and any use of such tools beyond copy-editing should be documented in the paper.
