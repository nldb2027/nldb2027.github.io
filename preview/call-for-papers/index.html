call for papers

n. phr.

An invitation to submit original, unpublished work to NLDB 2027, the 32nd International Conference on Applications of Natural Language to Information Systems, Bucharest, Romania.

next deadline 19 February 2027† Paper submission, 23:59 AoE
§ 1

Scope

Since 1995, NLDB has been where natural language processing meets information systems: databases and knowledge bases, search engines, and the applications built on them. We welcome contributions on theory, methods, resources and systems, from models of meaning to systems that answer questions over data, including work on large language models and their use with structured information.

topics include, but are not limited to

  1. Question answering, dialogue and interactive systems
  2. Interactivity and natural-language interfaces
  3. Generative models and large language models
  4. Information retrieval and text mining
  5. Discourse and pragmatics, sentiment analysis, argument mining
  6. Interpretability and model analysis in NLP
  7. Explainable AI
  8. AI safety and ethics
  9. Multimodality
  10. Social media and web data
  11. Efficient and low-resource methods
  12. Big data and scalability
  13. NLP applications

Papers on other topics at the intersection of language and information systems are welcome; if in doubt, write to the programme chairs.

§ 2

Tracks

Main track

Novel and significant research on theory, algorithms, applications, architectures and resources; surveys and discussion papers.

Industry track

Industrial-strength NLP work, case studies, reports on barriers to adoption, experience reports.

The same page limits and review process apply to both tracks.

§ 3

Paper types and length

TypeMaximum length
Full paper15 pages
Short paper11 pages
Demo paper6 pages

Page limits include references and appendices. Papers must use Springer’s lncs format and be submitted as pdf; submissions that do not follow the format may be rejected without review. The programme committee may accept a full paper as a short paper.

How to submit

Springer lncs guidelines and templates (LaTeX and Word).

Limits as at NLDB 2025; to be confirmed.

§ 4

Proceedings

Accepted papers are published by Springer in Lecture Notes in Computer Science. At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the conference and present the paper in person.

NLDB 2025 accepted 33 full, 19 short and 2 demo papers out of 120 submissions.
